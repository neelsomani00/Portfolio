Common questions

How do we start?

Reach out through the form on the contact page or any contact method listed. I'll send over a short brief to fill in, we'll agree on scope in a quick call, then work begins.

Can I reach you during the build?

Yes — I'm available between 3:00 PM and 10:30 PM during development, and at the same hours afterward for any follow-up questions.

How do I receive the finished website?

Once everything's finalized, I hand over the complete project as a file in whichever format you prefer.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Hosting is included. Domains are billed separately — but if you already own one, I'll happily connect it at no extra cost.
